# CubanCFCronology — User Guide

End-user guide for CubanCFCronology: a Spanish-language website about Cuban
science fiction and fantasy — a genre chronology, author profiles,
bibliography, awards, historical milestones and a news feed.

## What you'll find here

- **Autores (/autores):** Profiles of Cuban science fiction and fantasy authors
- **Obras (/obras):** Catalogue of published works (novels, short stories,
  poetry, plays, essays, chronicles, anthologies, comics)
- **Cronologia (/cronologia):** Timeline of the genre from 1999–2026
- **Noticias (/noticias):** News about Cuban SF/F
- **Fuentes (/fuentes):** All data sources used in the project
- **Licencia (/licencia):** Content and code licenses
- **Contacto (/contacto):** Corrections and collaboration channel

## How to use the site

### Browsing authors

Visit `/autores` to see the list of authors. Click on any author to view their
profile page with their published works.

### Browsing works

Visit `/obras` to browse the catalogue of works. You can filter by title, year,
type, and author. Click on any work to view its details.

### Exploring the chronology

Visit `/cronologia` to see works of type "chronicle" in an accessible semantic
timeline.

### Reading news

Visit `/noticias` for the latest news about Cuban science fiction and fantasy.

### Using the contact channel

Visit `/contacto` to submit corrections, report missing works or authors, or
propose collaborations. Your message will be reviewed and verified against
sources before being applied.

## Accessibility

The site is built with WCAG 2.1 AA compliance in mind:
- Semantic HTML with one `<h1>` per page
- Visible focus indicators
- Full keyboard navigation
- Meaningful alt text on images
- AA color contrast

## Frequently asked questions

**Q: Is the content free to use?**

A: Editorial content is CC BY-SA 4.0. You can share and adapt it as long as you
attribute the source and share under the same license.

**Q: Can I contribute?**

A: Yes! Use `/contacto` to send corrections, new works, or collaboration
proposals. All contributions are verified against sources before being applied.

**Q: Where does the data come from?**

A: All sources are documented in `/fuentes`. Priority sources include Korad
magazine, Secretos del Futuro anthology, and Tiempos Oscuros magazine.

**Q: Is there an API?**

A: Yes, read-only JSON endpoints at `/api/works.json` and `/api/authors.json`.
See `docs/API.md` for details.
